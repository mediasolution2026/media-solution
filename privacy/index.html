Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the website mediasolution.ge (the "Site"), operated by Media Solution LLC ("Media Solution," "we," "us," or "our"), registered in Tbilisi, Georgia. We are the data controller responsible for your personal data.

Contact: info@mediasolution.ge

2. What Information We Collect

Information you provide directly: name, email address, phone number, and message content when you submit our contact form.

Information collected automatically: technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and general usage data, collected via our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, for the purpose of delivering, securing, and maintaining the Site.

In the future, we may also collect analytics data (e.g. pages visited, time on site, referral source) through analytics cookies. We will update this policy and request your consent via a cookie banner before activating any such tools.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Contact form submissions: processed based on your consent and/or to take steps at your request prior to entering into a service arrangement (Art. 6(1)(a)/(b) GDPR).

Technical/security data via Cloudflare: processed based on our legitimate interest in keeping the Site secure and operational (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Future analytics cookies: will be processed based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), obtainable via a cookie consent mechanism.

4. Third Parties / Sub-Processors

We share limited technical data with Cloudflare, Inc., our hosting, content delivery, and security provider. Cloudflare may process visitor IP addresses and technical logs as part of delivering the Site. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. International Data Transfers

Because Cloudflare operates a global network, your data may be processed outside of Georgia or the European Economic Area. Where this occurs, transfers are safeguarded through Cloudflare's own compliance mechanisms, including Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions only for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and manage our business relationship, and no longer than 12 months, unless a longer period is required by law.

7. Your Rights

If you are located in the EU/EEA (or under applicable Georgian data protection law), you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion of your data, restrict or object to processing, request data portability, withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent), and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@mediasolution.ge.

8. Cookies

The Site currently does not use analytics or marketing cookies. Should this change, we will notify visitors via a cookie banner and update this section accordingly, listing each cookie's name, purpose, and duration.

9. Data Security

We rely on Cloudflare's security infrastructure (including DDoS protection and encrypted connections) to help protect data transmitted to and from the Site.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.

11. Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact us at info@mediasolution.ge — Media Solution LLC, Tbilisi, Georgia.
