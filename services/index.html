Services

Six disciplines. One connected model.

Media Solution's service model connects six complementary capabilities. Together, they take clients from strategic definition through content, distribution, optimization and organizational learning.

01

Strategy
and Consulting

We develop communication strategies that connect business objectives with audience needs, market realities and measurable outcomes. Our work begins with understanding the brand, its commercial priorities, competitive environment and target audience.

Core capabilitiesDigital and communication strategy, audience planning, campaign architecture, market-entry support, competitive analysis, channel strategy, annual and campaign-based planning.
02

Media Planning and Distribution

We plan, execute and optimize campaigns designed to reach the right audiences in the right environments, combining audience insight, platform expertise and continuous performance analysis.

Core capabilitiesMedia planning and buying, budget allocation, audience targeting, cross-channel distribution, campaign setup, optimization, vendor coordination across social, search, video, display, programmatic and local digital platforms.
03

Content
and Production

We create content for the platforms, audiences and environments in which it will appear — connecting creative craftsmanship with technology-enabled processes.

Core capabilitiesCreative concepts, campaign assets, social content, static design, video production, short-form content, adaptation, localization, production planning, shooting, editing and final delivery.
04

Influencer
Marketing

We build influencer collaborations that connect brands with audiences through credible voices and culturally relevant storytelling. Creators are assessed on relevance and quality, not follower counts alone.

Core capabilitiesInfluencer strategy, talent research and selection, negotiation, briefing, content coordination, execution, compliance monitoring and performance reporting.
05

Data Reporting and Measurement

We transform campaign data into clear insights that support better marketing decisions. Objectives and KPIs are defined before launch, and results are translated into practical recommendations.

Core capabilitiesMeasurement frameworks, campaign monitoring, cross-channel reporting, KPI analysis, performance evaluation, optimization recommendations and post-campaign learning.
06

Innovation and Client Enablement

We help clients understand, evaluate and implement emerging technologies and new approaches — identifying practical applications across strategy, production, media and measurement.

Core capabilitiesTechnology and platform consultation, new-format exploration, workflow development, client workshops, team education and implementation support.
