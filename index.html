About Media Solution

Experience meets a new generation.

Media Solution is a technology-driven communications and media agency helping ambitious brands achieve measurable growth through strategy, content and intelligent distribution.

Our Story

Founded in 2022, built for what's next.

Media Solution was established in 2022 by a group of experienced communications and media professionals who shared a common ambition: to build an agency where industry expertise could meet the energy, curiosity and innovative thinking of a new generation.

The founders wanted to create an environment where young talent would be encouraged to explore emerging technologies, challenge established approaches and develop a deeper understanding of modern communication. By bringing together experienced leadership and forward-thinking specialists, they built an agency designed to respond quickly to change while maintaining the strategic discipline and accountability expected by international brands.

Today, Media Solution combines experience, technology and fresh perspectives to help brands turn communication opportunities into measurable impact.

Mission
Our mission is to empower clients to understand, adopt, and effectively use the latest technological opportunities across the modern communications landscape — from creating relevant, high-quality content to ensuring its strategic and effective distribution.
  • Produce high-quality content that complies with relevant regulations and meets globally recognized creative standards.
  • Introduce innovative technologies and processes across content formats and content production.
  • Implement advanced approaches to media planning, placement and distribution.
  • Apply new tools and methodologies to communication strategy development.
  • Fulfil an educational role — helping clients understand emerging technologies and incorporate them confidently into their marketing ecosystems.
Vision
To help create a communications industry in which technology enhances human creativity, strategic intelligence and meaningful brand connections.

We aspire to lead the adoption of smarter, more responsible and more effective communication practices, enabling brands to navigate change confidently and build lasting relationships with their audiences.

Core Values

What we won't compromise on.

Innovation with Purpose

We adopt new technologies when they create meaningful value for brands and audiences.

Excellence through Consistency

We believe exceptional results come from high standards, attention to detail and continuous improvement.

Creative Craftsmanship

We combine creativity, technology and aesthetic integrity to produce relevant and effective content.

Curiosity and Learning

We continuously explore new ideas, platforms and methodologies and share that knowledge with our clients.

Accountability

We work transparently, take ownership of outcomes and make decisions based on evidence.

Services

Six disciplines. One connected model.

Media Solution's service model connects six complementary capabilities. Together, they take clients from strategic definition through content, distribution, optimization and organizational learning.

01

Strategy
and Consulting

We develop communication strategies that connect business objectives with audience needs, market realities and measurable outcomes. Our work begins with understanding the brand, its commercial priorities, competitive environment and target audience.

Core capabilitiesDigital and communication strategy, audience planning, campaign architecture, market-entry support, competitive analysis, channel strategy, annual and campaign-based planning.
02

Media Planning and Distribution

We plan, execute and optimize campaigns designed to reach the right audiences in the right environments, combining audience insight, platform expertise and continuous performance analysis.

Core capabilitiesMedia planning and buying, budget allocation, audience targeting, cross-channel distribution, campaign setup, optimization, vendor coordination across social, search, video, display, programmatic and local digital platforms.
03

Content
and Production

We create content for the platforms, audiences and environments in which it will appear — connecting creative craftsmanship with technology-enabled processes.

Core capabilitiesCreative concepts, campaign assets, social content, static design, video production, short-form content, adaptation, localization, production planning, shooting, editing and final delivery.
04

Influencer
Marketing

We build influencer collaborations that connect brands with audiences through credible voices and culturally relevant storytelling. Creators are assessed on relevance and quality, not follower counts alone.

Core capabilitiesInfluencer strategy, talent research and selection, negotiation, briefing, content coordination, execution, compliance monitoring and performance reporting.
05

Data Reporting and Measurement

We transform campaign data into clear insights that support better marketing decisions. Objectives and KPIs are defined before launch, and results are translated into practical recommendations.

Core capabilitiesMeasurement frameworks, campaign monitoring, cross-channel reporting, KPI analysis, performance evaluation, optimization recommendations and post-campaign learning.
06

Innovation and Client Enablement

We help clients understand, evaluate and implement emerging technologies and new approaches — identifying practical applications across strategy, production, media and measurement.

Core capabilitiesTechnology and platform consultation, new-format exploration, workflow development, client workshops, team education and implementation support.
